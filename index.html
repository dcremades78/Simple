03 · Who We're Talking To

Women who are done starting over
every Monday.

Simple's core audience isn't looking for motivation. They've tried diets. They've downloaded apps. They've done the routines. What they want is something that doesn't make them feel like they're failing.

Primary
Women 45–64
The 55–64 bracket is Simple's largest age group. Women at this stage respond to health messaging around sustainable habits, not extremes. They are active on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
Secondary
Women 35–45
Busy, digitally active, open to health tools that fit into real daily life. Strong on Instagram Reels and YouTube. Key platform for expanding reach without losing audience fit.
Psychology
The anti-dieter
Fatigued by restrictive apps. Doesn't want to count every calorie. Responds to "small steps" over "big transformations". Wants a coach, not a judge. Will subscribe if the product feels low-friction.

Platform priority for US 45+: Instagram and Facebook-native creators, YouTube integrations for depth. TikTok is secondary — the 55–64 core skews older than TikTok's primary audience. We build where the audience already lives.

06 · Creative Concepts

Content that earns the click
before asking for it.

Two campaign concepts, built for different entry points. One is the hero — designed for depth and emotional resonance. One is always-on and social-native. Both drive to the same place: Simple's quiz or landing page.

Hero concept
The Anti-Diet Diary
Instead of selling Simple as another weight loss solution, creators share everything they've already tried — and why it didn't stick. Then Simple enters not as a product, but as an answer to a question the audience already has.

"Every weight loss app made me feel like I was failing. This one feels more like a coach than a judge."

Three content formats per creator:

Format 1
"Things I'm done trying"
Extreme diets, calorie spreadsheets, the "start again Monday" cycle. Raw and relatable. High shareability. Simple is the natural alternative.
Format 2
"My no-drama health reset"
7-day documentary series. Small daily decisions guided by Simple: a better breakfast, a short walk, a chat with the AI coach. Real, not produced.
Format 3
"I asked women over 40 what actually worked"
Creator interviews women in her community. Simple sponsors the experiment and provides the tool that turns insights into a personalised plan.

The performance twist: all creator traffic lands on a micro-quiz — "What kind of anti-dieter are you?" — optimised for quiz completions, not just link clicks. Emotional entry point, performance measurement. The quiz is the bridge between creator content and subscription.

Always-on concept
The Monday Reset Club
A recurring weekly series where creators share one small, realistic decision — not a new diet, not a transformation. Just one better choice. Simple sponsors the series and provides the coaching layer behind each episode.

"Not a diet. Not a punishment. Just one better decision today."

Published every Monday across a roster of creators. Generates a constant stream of UGC reusable for paid social. An always-on brand presence without always-on production cost.

07 · The 3-Month System

We don't scale creators.
We scale what works.

One month tells you almost nothing. Three months gives you a learning loop: find what converts, cut what doesn't, put budget behind the winners. This is why we ask for a 3-month minimum commitment.

Month 1
Learn
8–12 creators activated across archetypes
Multiple hooks and formats tested per creator
Track: quiz starts, checkout starts, CPA trial
Identify top 30–40% of content by web conversion
Build initial UGC library for paid social
Month 2
Reallocate
Stop or reduce spend on low-converting creators
Scale budget to top-performing profiles
Launch first paid amplification with best UGC assets
Optimise landing page match per creator angle
Iterate hooks and formats based on CTR data
Month 3
Scale
Full budget allocated to proven creators and formats
Paid social amplification at scale with top UGC
Negotiate performance or CPA structures with best creators
Build lookalike audiences from converted quiz traffic
Report + recommendation for months 4+

In month 1, roughly 30% of creators will underperform. That's not a failure — that's the data. By month 3, that budget has been reallocated to the creators driving real web conversions. The system is designed to improve with time, not stay flat.

09 · Compliance

Weight loss + influencers
needs a clean framework.

The health and weight loss space is one of the most regulated verticals for influencer marketing. FTC in the US, ASA in the UK. We manage this from the start — not as an afterthought.

No medical claims. Creators speak from personal experience, not clinical outcomes. "This helped me build better habits" not "this cures X".
No guaranteed results. No "lose 20 lbs in 30 days" framing. No implied typical results without disclaimers.
No aggressive before/after. Journey content is fine. Dramatic transformation claims without context are not.
Clear disclosure on every post. #ad or Paid Partnership label on all sponsored content, per FTC guidelines.
Pre-approved claims guide. Simple provides approved language before production. Ramped reviews all content against it before publication.
Age-appropriate execution. Tone and messaging adapted to an adult audience, avoiding any content that could be harmful to vulnerable demographics.
